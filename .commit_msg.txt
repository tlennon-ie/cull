brand: rename to cull, full visual identity rollout

Renames the project from "image-classification-pipeline" to "cull"
across every text touchpoint, wires the logo into the dashboard +
README, and adds two branded surfaces (FAQ tab, About tab) plus
empty-state welcome cards. Origin remote updated to point at
github.com/tlennon-ie/cull.

Why "cull"
----------
Single-syllable verb that the audience already uses for this exact
ritual (photographers cull, ML engineers cull). Lower trademark risk
than the alternatives the strategy agent surveyed (Sieve, Pan, Sorted,
Cradle), and the product's positioning is "automating taste" rather
than "running a model" - cull names that workflow directly.

Tagline: "The curation engine for AI image datasets."
